## What it does

Delegates reading to a background agent that cites **primary sources** into one Markdown brief, with a short verdict and clear unknowns.

## When to reach for it

API/docs facts, vendor comparisons, "how does this work today", or an evidence pack ahead of a value case.

## Branches

- Technical
- Product / process
- Comparative
- Evidence pack (facts only; decisions stay with you)

## How it differs here

Same primary-source rule. Sunridge names product/vendor branches and will hand a too-large question to **orchestrate** instead of pretending one pass is enough.
