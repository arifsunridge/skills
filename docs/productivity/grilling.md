## What it does

`grilling` stress-tests a plan, product decision, or go-live before anyone acts. It maps the subject as a **design tree** and interviews you in **rounds**: each round asks the whole **frontier** (every decision whose prerequisites are settled), with a recommended answer on each question.

## When to reach for it

Type `/grilling`, or let the agent reach for it when a task needs alignment first. Prefer `/grill-me` when you want a user-only entry point the agent will not fire alone.

Use it for:

- Product calls and PRDs
- Value-case scope before drawing flows
- Go-live / cutover decisions
- Technical designs

## How it differs here

Sunridge grilling keeps Matt's rounds and frontier, and adds pressure for **owner**, **evidence**, **failure mode**, **manual residue**, and the **next artifact** (steps page, value-case page, research, orchestrate).

## It's working if

- A round arrives numbered, each with a `➡️` recommendation, answerable by number.
- Nothing in a round depends on another answer in the same round.
- Facts are looked up; decisions wait for you.
- It does not build until you confirm shared understanding.
