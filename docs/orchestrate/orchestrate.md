## What it does

Runs large work as a **time-boxed team of subagents**. You stay the conductor: cut jobs, pick model fit, launch the ready frontier, integrate before the clock dies.

## When to reach for it

Triggers: "use subagents", "fan out", "orchestrate", or a deadline ("finish in 40 minutes"). Shapes: codebase audits, broad research, multi-part builds, value cases, go-lives, multi-stakeholder packs.

## Why it exists

One context window cannot hold a whole audit or a multi-artifact pack. Orchestrate makes the cut explicit, assigns the right strength of model to each job, and prefers a partial honest hand-back over a late perfect plan.

## It's working if

- A roster exists before agents launch (job, deliverable, skill, model fit, deps).
- Parallel jobs do not share a write surface.
- The time box is named, and late in the box no new jobs start.
- The final artifact synthesises pointers; you are not asked to re-read every transcript.
